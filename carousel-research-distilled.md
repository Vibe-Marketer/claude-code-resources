# Carousel Research Distilled for CarouselForge

Key insights from "The Architecture of Attention" research, optimized for building CarouselForge.

---

## 1. The Core Psychology (Why Carousels Work)

**Zeigarnik Effect** is the foundational mechanic—people remember incomplete tasks. Each slide creates an "open loop" the brain must close, releasing dopamine in *anticipation* (not completion). This is slot machine psychology.

**Critical numbers:**
- Carousels get **3.1x more engagement** than single images
- **2x more likely to be saved** (saves = algorithm gold)
- 0.55% engagement rate vs 0.45% for static images
- Users decide in **2-3 seconds** on slide 1

---

## 2. The Slide 2 Secret (Build This In)

**Instagram sometimes shows Slide 2 first** to test engagement. Both slides 1 AND 2 must work as standalone hooks. No competitor accounts for this—CarouselForge should generate compelling hooks for *both* positions.

---

## 3. Seven Proven Formulas (Template These)

| Formula | Structure | Best For |
|---------|-----------|----------|
| **AIDA** | Attention → Interest → Desire → Action | Educational, lead gen |
| **PAS** | Problem → Agitate → Solve | Case studies, product marketing |
| **Before/After** | State → Process → Transformation | Testimonials, results |
| **Step-by-Step** | Number promise → One step per slide → Checklist | Tutorials (most saved) |
| **Hook-Value-CTA** | Bold hook → Aha moments → TL;DR → CTA | Quick tips |
| **4A Framework** | Actionable/Analytical/Aspirational/Anthropological | Content repurposing |
| **Story Arc** | Hook conflict → Rising action → Climax → CTA | Brand stories |

---

## 4. The Hook That Stops the Scroll

**Six hook types that work:**
1. **Question:** "Are you brushing your teeth wrong?"
2. **Shock/statistic:** "87% fail. Here's the 13%."
3. **Promise:** "Save this: 30 days of outfits"
4. **Step-by-step:** "From broke to 6-figure in 9 slides"
5. **Mistake:** "The #1 reason your reels get zero views"
6. **Curiosity:** "Wait until slide 7"

**Adding "Swipe →" increases swipe-through by 15-30%**—make this a default element.

---

## 5. Text Density Rules (Hard Constraints)

| Slide Type | Max Words |
|------------|-----------|
| Hook (Slide 1) | **3-12 words** |
| Content slides | **10-15 words** |
| CTA (Final) | **5-10 words** |

**One concept per slide, always.** This is non-negotiable.

---

## 6. Visual Design Non-Negotiables

### Typography
- Minimum text: **24px** (anything smaller = unreadable at scroll speed)
- Headlines: **36-64px**
- Title-to-body ratio: **2:1**
- Limit to **2-3 fonts** max

### Safe Zones
- Keep content in **center 80%** of canvas
- **250px padding** from all edges (Instagram UI covers edges)
- **60/40 rule**: 60% whitespace, 40% content

### Contrast
- Minimum **4.5:1 contrast ratio**
- Text over images needs **40-60% opacity overlays**

---

## 7. The Key Architectural Insight: Text-Image Separation

**No competitor does this.** Everyone generates text and images together, producing generic outputs. The research strongly validates a **split approach**:

1. **Text generation first** (apply persuasion frameworks, optimize copy)
2. **Image generation second** (style-referenced, consistent across all slides)
3. **Smart composition** (intelligent text placement on images)

### Benefits
- Text optimized independently for persuasion
- Visual consistency propagated across all 10 slides
- Can edit text without regenerating images
- Matches how human teams work (writer → designer)

---

## 8. Converting vs. Viral (Different Goals)

| Type | Optimizes For | CTA Style |
|------|---------------|-----------|
| **Viral** | Entertainment, social currency | "Save this", "Tag a friend" |
| **Converting** | Trust, value, action | "Comment GUIDE", "Link in bio" |

Converting carousels drive **3.4x higher purchase rates** than Reels. CarouselForge should offer both modes.

---

## 9. Offer Integration (Value-First Framework)

Top creators follow an **80/20 rule**: 80% pure value, 20% soft pitch.

### Natural Integration Techniques
- Case studies where the offer is part of the success story
- "Here's 3 of 10 strategies—full list in [offer]"
- CTA as *another free thing*: "Grab my free checklist"
- Conversational: "If you loved this, you'll love my newsletter"

### Never
- Jarring ad slide with different design
- Hard sell without value first

---

## 10. The Creator Workflow Pain Point

Current workflow takes **1-3 hours per carousel**:
1. Ideation/scripting (80% of time—biggest bottleneck)
2. Design execution in Canva (15-25 manual clicks per carousel)
3. Review/polish

Some creators spend **$1,500+/month on VAs** just for carousel creation. CarouselForge collapsing this to minutes is the value proposition.

## Summary: What CarouselForge Must Do Differently

1. **Text-image separation** — The moat no competitor has
2. **Dual-hook system** — Optimize slides 1 AND 2 as hooks
3. **Framework templates** — Encode AIDA, PAS, etc. into generation logic
4. **Hard text density limits** — Auto-enforce word counts
5. **60/40 whitespace rule** — Automatic layout with safe zones
6. **Swipe indicators by default** — 15-30% boost for free
7. **Style consistency propagation** — One hero style across all slides
8. **Converting vs. viral modes** — Different optimization paths
